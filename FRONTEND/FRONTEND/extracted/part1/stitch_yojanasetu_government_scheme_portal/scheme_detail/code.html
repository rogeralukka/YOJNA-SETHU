Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)

A central sector scheme to provide income support to all landholding farmers' families in the country to supplement their financial needs.

description Scheme Overview

Under the PM-KISAN scheme, an income support of ₹6,000 per year is provided to all farmer families across the country in three equal installments of ₹2,000 each every four months. The scheme aims to supplement the financial needs of farmers in procuring various inputs to ensure proper crop health and appropriate yields, commensurate with the anticipated farm income.

check_circle Eligibility Criteria

  • done Landholding farmer families with cultivable landholding in their names.
  • done Must be a resident of India.
  • done Aadhaar seeded bank account is mandatory.
  • close Institutional Landholders are not eligible.
